Trustees

Independent, board-led governance.

The Zenex Foundation is governed by an independent Board of Trustees who bring deep experience across education, finance, law and philanthropy — in South Africa and internationally.

Adv. Thandi Orleyn
Chair: Board of Trustees

Founding Trustee and chairperson of the Zenex Foundation. In 2025, the University of Fort Hare honoured Advocate Orleyn with an Honorary Doctor of Commerce for her contribution to South African public life and education.
