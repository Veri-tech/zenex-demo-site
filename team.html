Meet the team

The people behind the work.

A small, dynamic team of skilled professionals who collaborate and partner with government, service providers and school communities to improve teaching and learning outcomes for historically disadvantaged learners and schools across South Africa.

Leadership

Gail Campbell
Chief Executive Officer

Gail has been involved in developmental grant-making for over 15 years, with a particular emphasis on school education. With a social science background, she practiced as a social worker before joining ABSA as General Manager of Corporate Social Investment, where she was responsible for the ABSA Foundation. She serves on the boards of the National Research Foundation, Sci-Bono Discovery Centre, the Gauteng Education Development Trust and the KwaZulu-Natal Education Development Trust.

Dr Fatima Adam
Programme Director: Research & Evaluations

Fatima joined Zenex in 2012 after serving as programme director for Open Society Africa and in enrolment planning at the University of Johannesburg. She provides specialist input into all programme and project evaluations commissioned by the Foundation, develops its research strategy and agenda, and manages its research grants. She holds a PhD in Higher Education Transformation.

Thami Kubheka
Manager: Corporate Services & Finance

Thami joined the Zenex Foundation in September 2023. A professional accountant with over 20 years' experience across the public and private sectors, he has served as CFO of various organisations including Brand South Africa and the National Arts Council, and held executive roles in consulting, engineering and technology firms. He completed his articles with KPMG.
