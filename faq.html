FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Straight answers on what Zenex funds, where it works, and how projects are initiated — so the right organisations can self-select in.

What we do

What does the Zenex Foundation do?
We are an independent grant-maker established in 1995 to improve education outcomes in the public schooling system in South Africa. Our work revolves around advancing language and mathematics education through funding the implementation of programmes in schools, commissioning research and evaluations, and funding the development of learning and teaching resources.
Does Zenex Foundation fund research?
We commission research to better understand and solve key educational problems in language and mathematics. We also commission independent evaluation of all our funded programmes to deepen our learning, inform future programme development and influence policy and practice in the sector.

Where we work

Does Zenex Foundation work across the entire schooling system?
Strategy 2030 supports education improvement efforts in primary schools. We undertake work to support Foundation Phase literacy and numeracy and Intermediate Phase language and mathematics. The rationale of our focus is that language and mathematics are the bedrock of all learning and the basis upon which all subjects are built.
Where does Zenex Foundation roll out its projects?
We undertake projects primarily in four provinces: Gauteng, KwaZulu-Natal, Western Cape and Eastern Cape. These four provinces balance the urban-rural, socio-economic and language contexts. We may work nationally and in other provinces in partnership with the Department of Basic Education.

How we work

How does Zenex Foundation initiate its projects?
We are a proactive funder and conceptualise and co-design our projects in collaboration with educational experts and strategic partners. These projects are informed by internal and external research, as well as ongoing monitoring and evaluation. We generally approach experts and partners with initial project ideas rather than them approaching us.
How does Zenex Foundation roll out its projects?
Delivery is always through implementing agencies and researchers, selected through open calls for proposals. We put out calls for proposals and terms of reference on an ad-hoc basis to which suitable and interested individuals and agencies, primarily from the non-profit sector, respond.
Can individuals, schools, teachers and learners apply for funding?
We do not provide funding for proposals and requests received outside our calls for proposals. Through these calls, we fund multi-year projects, which means our funding is committed in advance and no funding is available for unsolicited requests. We also do not support individual schools, teachers, or learner bursaries.
Does Zenex Foundation fund school infrastructure or teaching resources?
We do not fund any form of infrastructural development, such as building science or computer laboratories or classrooms. We also do not supply other resources to schools such as school uniforms, stationery or food items. We do not support staff costs.
